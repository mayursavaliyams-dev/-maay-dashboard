How it works

Three stages, fully automated, fully observable.

1

Ingest the tape

Live index quotes, option chains and 5-minute highs/lows stream in and refresh the model continuously through market hours.

2

Score & gate

Strategies emit signals with a confidence score; the risk core gates them against capital, daily limits and charge-aware sizing.

3

Execute & track

Approved trades route to paper or live, then ride strike-walk and drawdown circuits until exit — every fill logged.
