This is where the price is, not a reason to buy. Each row is a leg trading near today's low right now: 0% means it is at the session low, 100% at the high. That is a fact about price and nothing more. A leg sits at its low because it has been falling — this platform's own 1,200-trade backtest put directional option buying at a profit factor of 0.94, a net loser, and the hero-zero base rate is still Unknown. The min day range filter is doing real work: without it the list fills with deep-ITM strikes whose entire day spanned 1% of their premium, which are not "at a low", they simply never moved. Highs and lows are confirmed extremes, so a live tick can sit fractionally outside them for one poll. Past PoP is a research-only bounce score from the leg's own intraday range and how close it is to the low; it is not a calibrated win rate and not a buy command. OI Mix and OI Analysis compare same-strike CE/PE change-OI as SUPPORT / CONFLICT / NEUTRAL / UNKNOWN; they are filter notes, not permission to trade. Buy Start appears only when the leg is near the day low and adjusted probability clears the gate; it is still paper/research context. Retest reads the live tick path: HELD_LOW means price touched the low zone, bounced, retested it, and stayed above failure. Result is a paper outcome tracker for shown setups: pending, target hit, or stop/failed-low. Setup folds freshness, liquidity, OI, trap checks and reward/risk into one research score. Truth checks time order: TRUE means today's high came after today's low; PENDING means the low came after the high, so the low-to-high path is not proven yet. Accuracy is TRUE / (TRUE + PENDING) for the visible filtered rows; UNKNOWN rows are excluded because their timestamps are incomplete.
This is perfect hindsight, not a strategy. Each row is the best buy-low-then-sell-high that was actually available in time order on that strike — the low must come before the high, so it is a pair someone could in principle have taken, unlike a bare high-minus-low. Nobody times both ends. Read it as the ceiling the day offered, useful for judging which strikes moved and when — never as a P&L you missed. Prices are 1-minute bars, so a lower intra-minute tick is not seen, and the archive is written by the capture loop, so today's rows can lag live by a few minutes.
