The Engine

Six instruments of a disciplined edge

Every module is built to protect capital first and compound it second.

ORB Signals

Opening-range breakout logic with volume confirmation and VWAP alignment to catch the day's first decisive move.

Gamma Blast

Detects expiry-day gamma acceleration and premium dislocations, sizing into asymmetric convex payoffs.

Risk Guard

Hard daily drawdown circuit, per-trade stops, and an auto-halt that resets cleanly the next session.

Backtest Engine

Replay years of expiry-Friday data with charge-accurate fills to validate any strategy before it touches the market.

Paper & Live Modes

Switch between simulated and real execution with one toggle. Default is paper, so you prove the edge first.

Charge-aware Sizing

Brokerage, STT, GST, and slippage are baked into every position-size calc — your P&L is net, never gross.
